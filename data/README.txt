STANZA DEMAND-TO-BED — SYNTHETIC PORTFOLIO DATASET
====================================================
All data is synthetic and created for a PM/AI prototype. It is NOT Stanza internal data.

Purpose:
Model the problem of matching qualified accommodation demand to suitable available inventory while balancing resident fit, booking propensity, inventory pressure and contribution economics.

Scale: 25,000 leads | 150 properties | 120 days/property | 112,394 interactions | 4,452 bookings | 11,013 sales actions

Recommended project flow:
1) Eligibility rules
2) Property-fit score
3) Booking propensity model
4) Inventory pressure score
5) Decision engine
6) Sales human-override loop
7) Experiment: default ranking vs Demand-to-Bed ranking

Important: columns named Synthetic_* or data generated here must never be presented as Stanza's real internal metrics.
